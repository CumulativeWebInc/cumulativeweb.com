The Architects

That Boy Hi Hat is the artist behind "Cyberpunk 2027" and the originator of Post-Trap Futurism — the genre the single exemplifies. Based in Frederick, Maryland, and managed within the Cumulative Web Inc catalog. Follow: Spotify · Instagram · X · TikTok.
Kokurcho produced "Cyberpunk 2027." He is 2x Billboard charting and BET-nominated, with production credits including Lil Durk, GloRilla, and Duwap Kaine. His production sets the single's futuristic, high-energy foundation.
Black Lansky is the co-producer and executive co-producer of "Cyberpunk 2027." He also designed and created the official anime visualization — the 42-second video where anime characters jump off Hi Hat's shirt. He leads Cumulative Web Inc as the label's driving creative force.
"Cyberpunk 2027" was recorded at Cue Recording Studios in Frederick, Maryland — the studio behind That Boy Hi Hat's catalog sessions and the birthplace of the Post-Trap Futurism sound.
