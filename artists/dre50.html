The sound

Dre50 makes afrobeats with Jamaican roots and Brazilian pull — records that sit at the crossover of dancehall, afrobeats, and hip hop without ever leaving the groove. His voice is melodic and half-sung, relaxed but precise, built for dancefloors, long drives, and open windows.
