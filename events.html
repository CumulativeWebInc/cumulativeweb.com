What CWI puts on

Cumulative Web Inc runs events as extensions of the catalog — not one-off parties, but formats. The template is Signal Sessions: a live session format piloted as a Rock-Rap Night around 183 Wildboi, engineered to be recorded, clipped, and distributed — the room is the studio, the audience is the proof. Release parties follow the same logic: every CWI single ships with a press release, a video treatment, and a live moment.

The current anchor on the calendar is Cyberpunk 2027 — That Boy Hi Hat’s October 20, 2026 single, produced by Kokurcho and executive co-produced by Black Lansky. The campaign spans two visualizations (anime and HD), an open model casting call, and live programming around release week. Watch this page and the press room for the event schedule.

Event types

Shows and sessions. Live performances and Signal Sessions-format recordings. Artists on the bill are CWI roster and invited collaborators; every session is captured for the label’s video and radio channels.

Release events. Single and project launches — listening sessions, video premieres, and the press cycle that ships with them. Releases are announced with lead time; the press room carries the official details.

Auditions and casting calls. Open calls for music videos, campaigns, and editorials — including the Cyberpunk 2027 video casting (18+, paid per shoot). See the models page for active calls.

Meetups. Community gatherings around the label’s scene — playlist curators, producers, visual artists, and the writers’ circle. Announced here and on the label’s social channels.

Get your event announced

Running something the CWI scene should know about? Use the contact page and pick the event-booking path: date, venue, capacity, lineup, and what makes it a CWI-fit. The team announces a short list — curated, not comprehensive.

Frequently asked

Where do CWI events happen?

The label is DMV-rooted (D.C., Maryland, Virginia) — most events happen in the region, with sessions engineered for broadcast so the stream is the front row. Live streams carry what the room can’t.

Are events all-ages?

It depends on the venue and the format — each announcement states the door policy. Casting calls are 18+.

How do I perform at a CWI event?

Through the event-booking path on the contact page: one track link, your live history, and the date you’re pitching for. The roster is curated; the bar is a real live show.

What’s on the horizon

The calendar builds around the release cycle. With Cyberpunk 2027 arriving October 20, 2026, the current season is release-week programming: the visualization premieres, the video production diary, listening sessions, and live appearances around the single. The HD and anime visualizations anchor the visual side; Radio 365 anchors the audio side.

Signal Sessions continues as the standing live format — the Rock-Rap Night pilot with 183 Wildboi set the template, and new sessions are booked per season. Release events follow each single; auditions and casting calls (including the Cyberpunk 2027 video call) are announced as they open. The rule: if it’s dated, it’s here — and if it’s here, the details are final.

For venues and promoters

CWI books selectively and announces early. The event-booking path on the contact page asks for exactly what the team needs: date, venue, capacity, lineup context, and the offer. What gets a yes: rooms that fit the catalog’s world, dates with real lead time, and deals that respect the artists. What gets a fast no: "exposure" offers and day-before asks.

Stay in the loop

Dated announcements live here first, then go out through the label’s channels: the blog for the story, the press room for the official record, live streams for the broadcast, and social (@cumulativeweb on YouTube, Instagram, X, and TikTok) for the day-to-day. If an event matters, it’s on at least three of those — check here first.

One rule for the calendar: if it’s dated, it’s here — and if it’s here, the details are final. No placeholders, no "TBA" headliners. The scene deserves better than maybes.
