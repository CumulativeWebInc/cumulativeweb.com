October 16, 2026 Single profile

That Boy Hi Hat's "Zooted Zone" — the catalog's hype record

Cover art for That Boy Hi Hat's single "Zooted Zone", via Cumulative Web Inc.

Washington, D.C., October 16, 2026 — Cumulative Web Inc profiles That Boy Hi Hat's single "Zooted Zone" — produced by Kokurcho and co-produced by Black Lansky, written by That Boy Hi Hat and Black Lansky, and mixed and mastered by Hybrid in Hagerstown, Maryland, where it was recorded in February 2023.

"Zooted Zone" is the hype record of the catalog: post-trap and underground rap at full volume, built for walkouts, fight nights, and the moment the room erupts. The label's sync catalog lists its scenes as club scene, nighttime driving, fight scene, and neon city — with use cases across film, television, games, sports, and boxing — and lyrical themes of ambition, pressure, and escape. Rights are cleared directly through Cumulative Web Inc.

The verified record: ISRC QZDA52303345, MusicBrainz recording MBID 1866a3dc-e7ea-4a0e-ac99-0d5397444ebf, streaming on Spotify. "Zooted Zone" is one of three That Boy Hi Hat singles selected for the label's November 2026 Discovery Mode campaign, and it is in rotation on the label's own Radio 365 station.
