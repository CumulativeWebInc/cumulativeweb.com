CWI Lab: Signal Sessions announced — pilot session spotlights 183 Wildboi's Rock-Rap Night

183 Wildboi in the studio. The pilot Signal Sessions session puts his Rock-Rap Night — "Stunt" and "Full Moon" — on the bench. Photo: CWI.

Cumulative Web Inc announces CWI Lab: Signal Sessions, a new weekly show where artist tracks get played live and run through CWI's audio analysis and verification tools on air — scores, ratings, and results published with every episode.

Pilot note. This is the pilot release. The show, its format, the pilot artist, and the spotlight tracks below are confirmed. The session date, the episode audio link, and the tool demoed are still landing and will be published with the episode out now release. Nothing else is claimed.

The format

Every Signal Sessions week runs five acts:

  1. Intro — the hosts introduce the song and the tool of the night.
  2. Play — the song plays, front to back.
  3. Live tool demo — the tool runs on the track, live on air.
  4. Rate it — the hosts rate the song and publish the tool's results.
  5. Your turn — a call for artists: submit your music to get run live on the tools in a future session.

The pilot session: 183 Wildboi — Rock-Rap Night

The first session puts CWI catalog artist 183 Wildboi in the spotlight with "Stunt" and "Full Moon" — both co-produced with Black Lansky as part of the Rock-Rap Revival: guitars and drums crashing straight into rap cadence.

Label verification: both spotlight tracks are verified CWI label releases — Record Label Cumulative Web Inc, each "successfully processed and delivered to stores": "Stunt" (UPC 198094066528 · ISRC QZMEP2463049 · released 2024-05-19) and "Full Moon" (UPC 197147974261 · ISRC QZNWS2289104 · released 2022-08-26). Verification source: DistroKid label dashboards supplied by Black, verified 2026-09-29. Label control confirmed; master/publishing ownership splits remain under attorney review.

When the episode ships, this page's episode out now follow-up will carry the episode link, the tool demoed, and the on-air results.
