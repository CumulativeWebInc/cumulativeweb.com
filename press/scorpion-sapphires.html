That Boy Hi Hat's "Scorpion & Sapphires" — two recordings, one dark record

Cover art for That Boy Hi Hat's "Scorpion & Sapphires", via Cumulative Web Inc.

Washington, D.C., October 16, 2026 — Cumulative Web Inc profiles That Boy Hi Hat's "Scorpion & Sapphires" — the alternative-rap cut that exists as two verified Spotify recordings, titled "Scorpion & Sapphires" and "Scorpions & Sapphires" (the album cut on The Alternative Theory).

The record is the most menacing cut in the That Boy Hi Hat catalog: nocturnal, cinematic, dangerous, and anthemic. The label's sync catalog lists its scenes as fashion film, nighttime driving, and club scene, with lyrical themes of danger, luxury, and allure — and rights cleared directly through Cumulative Web Inc.

On the record, honestly: producer, studio, and release-date credits for this recording are not yet on file and are being confirmed with the label; no MusicBrainz recording ID is on file. What is verified and on file: ISRC QZDA72377310 and both live Spotify recordings, linked below. "Scorpion & Sapphires" is one of three That Boy Hi Hat singles selected for the label's November 2026 Discovery Mode campaign.
