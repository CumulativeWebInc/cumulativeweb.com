That Boy Hi Hat — “Cyberpunk 2027”: Anime Visualization Premieres Ahead of October 20 Single

October 8, 2026 — Cumulative Web Inc

Cumulative Web Inc has premiered the official anime visualization for “Cyberpunk 2027,” the new single from That Boy Hi Hat, out worldwide on October 20, 2026. The 42-second visual — designed and created by label founder Black Lansky — premiered October 5, 2026 on the Cumulative Web Inc YouTube channel (youtu.be/YNMsgkXhANU).

The single was produced by Kokurcho — the 2x Billboard-charting, BET Awards nominated producer behind records for Lil Durk, GloRilla and Duwap Kaine — and co-produced by Black Lansky. It was recorded at Cue Recording Studios and arrives via Cumulative Web Inc (UPC 700563274374).

“Cyberpunk 2027 is That Boy Hi Hat’s dive into a neon-drenched future — produced by 2x Billboard-charting, BET-nominated Kokurcho (Lil Durk, GloRilla) and co-produced by Black Lansky at Cue Recording Studios. The track fuses post-trap futurism with cinematic cyberpunk soundscapes, built for both the underground and the algorithm,” the label said in its Spotify editorial pitch.

Fans can pre-save the single now on the official DistroKid Hyperfollow page (distrokid.com/hyperfollow/thatboyhihat/cyberpunk-2027). The track is already spinning on CWI’s Radio 365. Full release details, credits and the visualizations live on the official landing page at cumulativeweb.com/cyberpunk-2027.html.

Sync licensing for film, TV and games is handled directly by the label at cumulativeweb.com/sync/.
