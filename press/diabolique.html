October 16, 2026 Single profile

That Boy Hi Hat's "Diabolique" — nocturnal single with direct sync clearance

Cover art for That Boy Hi Hat's single "Diabolique", via Cumulative Web Inc.

Washington, D.C., October 16, 2026 — Cumulative Web Inc profiles That Boy Hi Hat's single "Diabolique" — the alternative-rap/post-trap record produced by Hybrid and co-produced by Black Lansky, recorded at Cue Recording Studio in Arlington, Virginia.

Released as a single on July 3, 2026, "Diabolique" is the catalog's nocturnal cut: dark, seductive, and cinematic, built for late-night scenes, fashion film, and neon-city sequences. The label's sync catalog lists its moods as dark, brooding, cinematic, hazy, cool, and understated, with lyrical themes of temptation, danger, and allure — and rights cleared directly through Cumulative Web Inc, with no chain of title to untangle.

The verified record: ISRC QZ8EF2666377, MusicBrainz recording MBID 4599cf5e-96c2-4508-b15c-93da205bfca5, streaming on Spotify. "Diabolique" is one of three That Boy Hi Hat singles selected for the label's November 2026 Discovery Mode campaign, and it is in rotation on the label's own Radio 365 station.
