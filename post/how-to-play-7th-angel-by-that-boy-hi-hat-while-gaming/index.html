How to Play 7th Angel While Gaming

By Cumulative Web Inc · October 19, 2023

“7TH ANGEL” by That Boy Hi Hat is a great song to listen to while gaming. It's upbeat and energetic — it'll help you get pumped up for your game.

On PS5

Open Spotify on your PS5, search “7th Angel That Boy Hi Hat,” and start playback. Use the quick menu to control volume without leaving your game.

On Xbox Series X|S

Launch the Spotify app from the Microsoft Store, find the track, and play it in the background while gaming.

On Nintendo Switch

Use your phone or a second device for Spotify playback while gaming on Switch — pair a Bluetooth speaker for the full experience.
