By Cumulative Web Inc · October 01, 2023

The DMV (DC, Maryland, Virginia) is producing some of the most exciting new music in hip hop and alternative rap. Here are 7 artists you need to know.

That Boy Hi Hat

Leading the charge with Post-Trap Futurism — alternative rap that blends introspective lyricism with atmospheric production. Tracks like “7th Angel” and “Zooted Zone” showcase his range.

The New Wave

From Baltimore to the Maryland suburbs to Northern Virginia, a new generation of artists is redefining what DMV music sounds like. Alternative rap, lofi, dark trap — the region's sonic palette has never been wider.
