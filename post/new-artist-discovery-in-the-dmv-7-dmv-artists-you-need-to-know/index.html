DMV Hip Hop History

The DMV's musical legacy runs deep. Go-go — pioneered by Chuck Brown — gave the region its rhythmic foundation. In hip hop, artists like Wale put DC on the map nationally. Baltimore club music created an entirely new genre. The hardcore scene produced legendary bands.

Today's artists stand on these shoulders while forging new paths. The 7 featured here represent the current wave — but they're part of a much longer story.
