How That Boy Hi Hat is Becoming the Next Alternative Hip Hop Artist

By Cumulative Web Inc · October 20, 2023

THAT BOY HI HAT is known for his unique sound, his introspective lyrics, and his energetic live performances. He's also a social media savvy artist, using platforms like Instagram, Twitter, TikTok, YouTube, and Spotify to connect with fans and promote his music.

From breakout singles like “7th Angel” and “Zooted Zone” to deep catalog cuts, That Boy Hi Hat has built a sound that's entirely his own — blending hip hop, soul, and atmospheric production into something critics call Post-Trap Futurism.

Taking Over Social Media

Hi Hat's rise isn't just about the music — it's about connection.

His energetic live performances and consistent social presence have built a loyal following across every major platform.

The Music

That Boy Hi Hat's catalog spans alternative rap, post-trap, and atmospheric hip hop. From the dark cinematic scope of "7th Angel" to the hypnotic energy of "Zooted Zone" to the reflective anthem "Doves & Diamonds" — each release showcases a different facet of his artistry.

His production partnerships — with Jeck Da General, Kokurcho, and Black Lansky — have created a signature sound that's immediately recognizable: introspective lyrics over atmospheric, hard-hitting production.

What's Next

With a growing catalog, increasing playlist placements, and a dedicated fanbase, That Boy Hi Hat is positioned as one of the most exciting voices in alternative hip hop. The takeover is just getting started.
