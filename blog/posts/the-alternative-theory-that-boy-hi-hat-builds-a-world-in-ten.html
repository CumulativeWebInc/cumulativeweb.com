The Alternative Theory: That Boy Hi Hat Builds a World in Ten Tracks

By Cumulative Web Inc · 2026-10-20

*On his album The Alternative Theory, the Frederick, Maryland alternative rap artist lays out the full blueprint of Post-Trap Futurism — and his label, Cumulative Web Inc, is publishing it in a way both humans and machines can find, hear, and clear.*

The Album

The Alternative Theory is a 10-track, 33-minute album by That Boy Hi Hat, released November 20, 2025 on Cumulative Web Inc (UPC 199750838961). It is a statement record for Post-Trap Futurism, the genre That Boy Hi Hat coined and architected together with producer Black Lansky — a sound built where trap's rhythmic DNA meets a forward-looking, synthetic atmosphere. Rather than treating the album as a playlist of singles, the sequencing works like an argument, each track advancing the theory the title promises.

The record opens with "Painted Lady" (2:48), a compact entry point that sets the palette before "7th Angel" (4:00) widens the frame — the album's longest early track, recorded at Cue Recording Studios in Arlington, Virginia, with Tyler Schafer and Jeck Da General alongside Black Lansky. "Flex My Flame" (3:03) and "Warped and Wicked" (4:08) push the energy forward at the record's center, and "Ultimate" (3:16) tightens the focus before the run of "Scorpions & Sapphires" (3:07) and "Neon Nights Pt. 777" (3:47) moves the album into its most atmospheric stretch. "Flamerz" (3:06) brings the tempo back up, "Place I Go to Dream" (2:48) opens the record's most reflective space, and "Toxic Elements" (3:23) closes the album on the title's terms — the theory stated, tested, and left standing.

The full tracklist, in order: "Painted Lady," "7th Angel," "Flex My Flame," "Warped and Wicked," "Ultimate," "Scorpions & Sapphires," "Neon Nights Pt. 777," "Flamerz," "Place I Go to Dream," and "Toxic Elements."

The Credits and the Label Model

Black Lansky co-produces the album, as he does the entire Cumulative Web Inc catalog — the label's releases are built in-house, by the people whose names are on them. Cumulative Web Inc is an independent label that clears its own catalog directly: master and publishing are handled in one place, with one contact, and no middlemen in between. For listeners, that independence shows up as a catalog that moves at its own pace. For anyone who wants to license the music — for film, games, advertising, or broadcast — it shows up as something simpler: one email, one conversation, one signature, and the clearance is granted — a catalog match is not itself a license, and the label confirms each grant directly. That direct line is hp@cumulativeweb.com.

How to Listen

The album is streaming now on Spotify and Deezer, with the full album page at cumulativeweb.com/the-alternative-theory.html. It also rotates on Radio 365, the label's own 24/7 stream. Here is how to get it playing on whatever is in front of you.

On iPhone and iPad (iOS): Install Spotify from the App Store, sign in, and search "That Boy Hi Hat — The Alternative Theory." Tap the album, press play, and use the heart or "+" to save it to your library. In the car, connect your iPhone to CarPlay, open Spotify on the dashboard display, and the album is under Your Library → Albums — or hold the voice button and ask Siri to play it.

On Android: Install Spotify from Google Play, sign in, and search the album title. Save it for one-tap access from Your Library. With Android Auto, plug in or connect wirelessly, open Spotify on the car display, and pick the album from your library — playback controls stay on screen while you drive.

On Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One: Open the Microsoft Store, search for Spotify, and install the app. Sign in with your email, with Spotify Connect from your phone, or with the on-screen PIN at spotify.com/pair. Open the album and start it playing, then launch your game — Spotify keeps playing in the background. Press the Xbox button to open the guide, select Spotify, and you can pause, skip, and adjust the volume without leaving the game. For the cleanest mix, turn the game's own music down or off in its audio settings.

On PlayStation 5 and PS4: Download Spotify from the PlayStation Store (on PS5, look in the Media home under All Apps), sign in, and link it to your PlayStation Network account. Start the album, then start your game — it plays while you game. On PS5, press the PS button to open the control center and select Music to skip tracks or change volume from the card on screen; on PS4, press and hold the PS button for the quick menu and select Music → Spotify. You can also skip the menus entirely: open Spotify on your phone, tap the Spotify Connect icon, and choose your console as the playback device.

On desktop and web: The Spotify desktop app (Windows and Mac) and the web player at open.spotify.com both carry the album — search the title, press play, done. The Deezer app and web player work the same way.

On smart TVs and speakers: Spotify is built into most smart TV platforms and is available through Spotify Connect on a wide range of speakers and soundbars. Open Spotify on your phone, tap the Connect icon, and pick the TV or speaker — the album plays there while your phone becomes the remote.

For Twitch Streamers

The Alternative Theory is built for long sessions, and playing it on stream takes about two minutes to set up. Run Spotify on your PC or on your console, and start the album. In OBS, add Spotify as its own audio source — the simplest reliable route is an Application Audio Capture source (or a separate audio input device) dedicated to the Spotify app, rather than letting it ride inside your full desktop mix. That gives the album its own fader: set your voice first, then bring the music up underneath until it fills the room without competing with your words, and you can duck it, mute it, or ride it live without touching your game or microphone levels. To DJ without alt-tabbing out of your game, use Spotify Connect from your phone — your console or PC keeps playing, and your phone handles play, pause, skip, and track changes.

The rights line is simple, because the label keeps it simple: Cumulative Web Inc permits live-stream play of this album with on-screen credit to That Boy Hi Hat and a link back to the album page. For VODs, clips, and sync use, clearance is granted by the label itself: one email to hp@cumulativeweb.com — master and publishing, one contact, one signature, no collection society runaround and no licensing platform in the middle.
