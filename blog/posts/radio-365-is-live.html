What launched

Radio 365 went live October 4, 2026 — Cumulative Web Inc’s own 24/7 streaming station, carrying the full catalog around the clock: alternative rap, post-trap futurism, dark lofi, rap rock, cyberpunk. Eight masters opened the rotation on day one, including Dre50’s "Pix," five King Ahkeem singles ("Embiid," "Bluefaces 3.0," "Star," "Super Solid," "Element"), and Fleekz’s "2K23 Corners" with official cover art. ("Be Mad" is held for artwork.)

The station is the owned channel at the center of the label’s flywheel. Releases premiere on Radio 365; the station feeds the discovery campaigns; the campaigns feed the catalog’s streaming numbers. No algorithms guessing taste, no ad breaks, no payola queue — the label’s records, sequenced for long listening sessions, 24 hours a day.

The rotation

The backbone is That Boy Hi Hat’s catalog — "Zooted Zone" (307,439 lifetime plays), "Doves & Diamonds," "Shaka Zulu," "Diabolique," "Flamerz," "7th Angel" — plus 183 Wildboi’s rap-rock lane ("Stunt"), King Ahkeem’s all-trap catalog, Dre50’s afrobeats-Jamaican-Brazilian fusions, and Black Lansky’s Lofi 2.0. New singles join on release: "Cyberpunk 2027" enters rotation October 20, 2026.

Listen at cumulativeweb.com/radio — browser-based, no account. The launch press release is in the press room.

Frequently asked

Is Radio 365 free?

Yes — no account, no app, no subscription. It’s a discovery channel; the label wants the audience, not the ticket revenue.

How is this different from a playlist?

A playlist is a list; a station is programming. Radio 365 sequences the catalog for sessions — premieres, takeovers, and release-week specials on top of the 24/7 loop.

Can I get my music on the station?

The rotation is the CWI catalog plus invited collaborators. Roster artists rotate automatically; collaborations go through the contact page.

What the launch proved

Eight masters on day one proved the premise: the catalog is deep enough to program a station. But the launch proved something else too — that the label’s release discipline compounds. Every record in the rotation arrived with cover art to spec, verified credits, and platform links, because the 10-step rubric demanded it. A station is only as good as its metadata; CWI’s was ready.

The next proof point is "Cyberpunk 2027" entering rotation on October 20, 2026 — the first single to premiere on Radio 365 as part of a full campaign (visualizations, casting, press, live programming). Watch how a release moves when the station, the blog, and the press room all fire at once. That’s the flywheel, and it’s just getting started.

How to listen

Open cumulativeweb.com/radio and hit play — the stream runs in the browser on desktop or mobile, no account, no app. Leave it on: the loop is sequenced for long sessions, and special programming (premieres, takeovers, release-week specials) is announced on the page and the label’s social channels.

Does Radio 365 have DJs?

The station is programmed, not hosted — the catalog sequenced for flow. Live voice programming arrives with special events; the day-to-day is the music, uninterrupted.

Listen at cumulativeweb.com/radio — and if the station’s programming moves you, the discovery shelves are where each record’s story lives.

The station’s first campaign premiere — "Cyberpunk 2027" on October 20, 2026 — will show what the flywheel does at full power: the single, the visualizations, the press cycle, and the 24/7 rotation moving as one. Tune in and watch a release land in real time.

The bigger picture

Radio 365 is the owned channel in the label’s five-front operation: the playlist engine, Discovery Mode measurement, the owned-channel flywheel, the content funnel, and release cadence — running in parallel, at full throttle. The station is the flywheel’s hub: every front feeds it, and it feeds every front. October 2026 is the first month all five run at once.
