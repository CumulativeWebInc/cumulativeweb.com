Zooted Zone: That Boy Hi Hat's hype record, from Kokurcho and Black Lansky

By Cumulative Web Inc · October 16, 2026

Cover art for That Boy Hi Hat's single "Zooted Zone" — Cumulative Web Inc.

"Zooted Zone" is the hype record of the That Boy Hi Hat catalog — the track built to open the show, walk out the fighter, or score the moment the room erupts. Where the catalog's nocturnal cuts go low and dark, "Zooted Zone" goes high voltage: post-trap and underground rap at full volume.

The record was produced by Kokurcho and co-produced by Black Lansky, written by That Boy Hi Hat and Black Lansky, and mixed and mastered by Hybrid in Hagerstown, Maryland. It was recorded in Hagerstown in February 2023 — an early catalog cut that has aged like fuel.

The verified record

The single carries ISRC QZDA52303345 and a verified MusicBrainz recording MBID (1866a3dc-e7ea-4a0e-ac99-0d5397444ebf). It is live on Spotify, with its full platform presence linked from the label's song page at cumulativeweb.com/zooted-zone.html. It is in rotation on Radio 365, the label's own station.

The sync profile

This is a walkout record. The label's sync catalog lists its scenes as club scene, nighttime driving, fight scene, and neon city — with use cases across film, television, games, sports, and boxing. Its lyrical themes are ambition, pressure, and escape; its moods run hype, aggressive, anthemic, dark, electric, and punchy.
