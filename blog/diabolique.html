Diabolique: That Boy Hi Hat's nocturnal single, cut at Cue Recording Studio

By Cumulative Web Inc · October 16, 2026

Cover art for That Boy Hi Hat's single "Diabolique" — Cumulative Web Inc.

"Diabolique" is the late-night record in the That Boy Hi Hat catalog. Released as a single on July 3, 2026, it sits in the lane the label calls alternative rap and post trap: dark, seductive, cinematic, and unhurried. It plays like the 2 a.m. part of the setlist — the record that closes the room down.

The single was produced by Hybrid and co-produced by Black Lansky, and it was recorded at Cue Recording Studio in Arlington, Virginia. That room is the through-line on the catalog's heaviest records, and it shows here: the track carries a halftime-trap pulse with the room tone of a real studio behind it.

The verified record

The single carries ISRC QZ8EF2666377 and a verified MusicBrainz recording MBID (4599cf5e-96c2-4508-b15c-93da205bfca5). It is live on Spotify, with its full platform presence linked from the label's song page at cumulativeweb.com/diabolique.html. It is in rotation on Radio 365, the label's own station.

The sync profile

This is a sync-built record. The label's sync catalog lists its scenes as fashion film, nighttime driving, neon city, and title sequence; its lyrical themes are temptation, danger, and allure; its mood tags run dark, brooding, cinematic, hazy, cool, and understated. "Diabolique" is one of three That Boy Hi Hat singles — alongside "Scorpion & Sapphires" and "Zooted Zone" — selected for the label's November 2026 Discovery Mode campaign in Spotify for Artists.
