# Diabolique is the October Discovery Mode control

That Boy Hi Hat's single **"Diabolique"** — produced by Hybrid and co-produced by Black Lansky — is Cumulative Web Inc's October Discovery Mode control.

Discovery Mode puts the track in front of listeners inside Spotify's programming, and October's campaign is the measurement baseline CWI is running the full month against: streams, saves, playlist adds, and listener conversion, tracked week over week. The campaign is confirmed running in Spotify for Artists.

The opening signal is already on the board: "Diabolique" won its DistroKid Spotlight matchup 38–2 (verified from the live DistroKid vote page on 2026-10-03; re-checked 2026-10-04). Spotlight votes reset weekly, and winning feeds DistroKid's own editorial playlists.

"Diabolique" is the spearhead of the catalog push — the track everything else in the October program is measured against. Its machine-readable record, with the verified Spotify track ID, is published in the [CWI learning index](https://cumulativewebinc.github.io/cwi-learn/tracks/diabolique.html).
