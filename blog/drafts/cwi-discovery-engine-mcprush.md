# CWI Discovery Engine Joins mcprush — Our Music Catalog Meets the AI Marketplace

The CWI Discovery Engine — our MCP server that puts the entire Cumulative Web Inc music catalog inside any AI app — is now listed on [mcprush](https://mcprush.com/cumulativewebinc/cwi-discovery-engine-mcp), the marketplace for MCP servers and agent skills.

## What this means

Any developer building with AI can now find our server on mcprush and connect it to their AI app in one command. Once connected, their AI can search our catalog, pull track details, look up artists, run semantic searches across 30+ tracks, and explore the full knowledge graph — all through 7 purpose-built tools: search_catalog, get_track, get_artist, sync_search, semantic_search, get_graph, and get_release.

## The AI-to-AI angle

This is distribution most artists don't have. When someone asks their AI assistant for music — a track for a film scene, a new artist in post-trap futurism, what's playing on an independent station — our catalog is right there in the AI's context, ready to surface. Every connected AI becomes a discovery channel for CWI music.

## Radio 365, built in

The Discovery Engine also carries Radio 365 — our 365-day independent station streaming at [cumulativeweb.com/radio.html](https://cumulativeweb.com/radio.html). Connected AIs can tune in and surface what's playing, turning every agent into a passive listener.

## For developers

Find us on mcprush: [mcprush.com/cumulativewebinc/cwi-discovery-engine-mcp](https://mcprush.com/cumulativewebinc/cwi-discovery-engine-mcp). The server is also registered on the official MCP Registry as `io.github.CumulativeWebInc/cwi-discovery-engine`, and our [llms.txt](https://cumulativeweb.com/llms.txt) documents every discovery surface.

Build something with it. The catalog is waiting.
