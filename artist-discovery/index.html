Artist Discovery

Artist discovery at Cumulative Web Inc is infrastructure, not an afterthought. The label runs a dedicated discovery engine — curated shelves across alt rap, lofi, underground hip hop, platform-specific lanes, and DMV artists — each one a doorway into the catalog. Twenty-four shelves, one movement: post-trap futurism and the independent artists building it.

The discovery loop is simple: pick a shelf, meet the anchor record, then follow the posts into the full story — production credits, playlist evidence, and streaming links, all verified. New shelves open as the catalog grows; every page below was rebuilt in October 2026 at its original URL.

Start anywhere. The flagship shelf is Zooted Zone — the record that proved the model. For the label’s own story, see Cumulative Web; for the region, New Artist Discovery in the DMV.

Frequently asked

How does CWI artist discovery work?

Through curated shelves like the ones above, the Signal Sessions live pilot program, and the machine-readable discovery engine (MCP server) that lets AI agents query the catalog directly.

Which shelf should I start with?

New to the label: Zooted Zone. Into darker sounds: 7th Angel. Emo rap: Broken Hearts Club. Regional: New Artist Discovery in the DMV.

Are these pages kept current?

Yes — every shelf was rebuilt in October 2026 at its original URL and carries a machine-readable last-modified date.
