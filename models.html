Open casting, real shoots

Cumulative Web Inc casts real people for its music videos, campaigns, and editorials — an open call, not a closed rolodex. The label’s visual work (music videos, visualizers, campaign editorials) needs faces that fit the world: post-trap futurism, cyberpunk, dark luxury, alt-rap authenticity. If that’s you, this is the door.

The active call is the Cyberpunk 2027 video casting — That Boy Hi Hat’s October 20, 2026 single. The brief: adults 18 and over, all looks, paid per shoot ($50–$150 negotiable depending on role and shoot length), with LGPD-compliant consent for Brazil-based talent. Likeness is AI-rendered into the final video treatment — nothing explicit, full consent paperwork, professional set.

What the work looks like

Music videos. The label treats videos as cinema — "Doves & Diamonds" was shot as a film with drone footage over Hagerstown, Maryland. Shoots are directed, scheduled, and paid. You’ll know the call time, the location, the wardrobe, and the rate before you commit.

Campaigns and editorials. Single campaigns, playlist pushes, and brand editorials need stills and short-form video. These are usually half-day shoots with a defined brief.

Live and session appearances. Signal Sessions recordings and release events sometimes need audience and atmosphere casting — credited, never "exposure-only."

How to apply

Use the contact page and pick the model-call path: your name, age (18+), location, availability, and two recent photos (one portrait, one full-length — phone photos are fine). For the Cyberpunk 2027 call, mention it by name. The casting desk replies to every serious application; if you’re a fit, you’ll get the brief, the rate, and the shoot date.

Frequently asked

Do I need experience?

No. Presence and reliability beat portfolios — most of the label’s favorite faces had never been on a set before. Follow direction, hit your call time, and you’ll get booked again.

What does it pay?

Shoots are paid per shoot — the Cyberpunk 2027 call is $50–$150 negotiable by role. Rates are agreed in writing before the shoot day. No unpaid "exposure" bookings, ever.

Where are shoots held?

The label is DMV-based; most shoots are in the D.C.–Maryland–Virginia region, with select Brazil-based production under LGPD consent. Travel expectations are stated in the brief.

Will my likeness be used in AI-generated content?

Only with your explicit, written consent for the specific project — as with the Cyberpunk 2027 video, where likeness is AI-rendered into the final treatment. You review and approve before anything publishes.
