The Jamaican Alt-Rap Lane

Jamaica's footprint on global music runs through dancehall — the sound the island exported to the world and the rhythmic language half of hip-hop's production vocabulary is built on. The dancehall-rap intersection is a real lane: Jamaican MCs working over hip-hop and trap infrastructure, hip-hop producers building on dancehall's drums, and a generation of artists who grew up fluent in both. Fleekz's catalog genres — hip-hop, dancehall, trap — sit at exactly that intersection. This is analysis, not biography: the page claims no sound for "2K23 Corner$" beyond what the catalog's genre tags say.
