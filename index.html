Cumulative Web Inc — Independent Record Label

An independent label that owns its rails

Cumulative Web Inc is an independent record label built like a technology company. The roster — That Boy Hi Hat, 183 Wildboi, King Ahkeem, Dre50, Fleekz, and the Black Lansky solo catalog — runs on infrastructure the label owns outright: Radio 365, a 24/7 streaming station; a film unit that treats music video as cinema; the artist discovery engine; a press desk with verified-fact releases; and a software estate (SANQA) that productizes the machine’s own instruments.

The catalog’s center of gravity is post-trap futurism — That Boy Hi Hat’s lane, built out of the DMV: hip-hop’s street narratives rewired through synthetic, forward-leaning production. The flagship records tell the story: "Zooted Zone" (307,439 lifetime Spotify plays, No. 30 on New Rap Hits), "Shaka Zulu" (No. 21), "Doves & Diamonds" (No. 31, with an official film), and "Diabolique" — the 2026 single spearheading the October Discovery Mode campaign. Every track in the catalog carries Black Lansky as co-producer.

What’s happening now

Cyberpunk 2027 — That Boy Hi Hat’s October 20, 2026 single, produced by Kokurcho and executive co-produced by Black Lansky — is the current campaign: two official visualizations (anime and HD), an open model casting call, and release-week programming across radio and live. The blog carries the running story; the press room carries the official record.

For the scene: open casting for videos and campaigns, events including the Signal Sessions format, services for independent artists (playlist evidence, sync readiness), and artist signup for projects that belong in the catalog’s world.

The machine behind the music

What makes CWI different isn’t just the records — it’s the machine that ships them. The 10-step release rubric (80+ to ship, no exceptions) gates every release. The press desk publishes verified-fact releases with NewsArticle schema. The blog ships every post with a JSON twin for AI readers. The discovery engine (MCP server, listed on mcprush) makes the catalog queryable by any agent. The software estate and SANQA suite productize the instruments. This site is the machine’s front door — and every page on it is scored, schema’d, and kept current.

Cumulative Web Inc (CWI) is an independent record label based in the Washington DC metropolitan area, specializing in alternative rap and post-trap futurism. Founded by producer Black Lansky, the label develops artists, produces records, and licenses music for film, television, and games.

The Artist Roster

The CWI roster brings together some of the most distinctive voices in independent music. That Boy Hi Hat leads the flagship catalog with a genre-defying sound that has earned millions of streams. 183 Wilboi fuses rock energy with rap cadence. Jamaican artist Dre50 brings dancehall and afrobeats heat with records like "Pix" and "Vai Vem Vem." Greg Porn, King Ahkeem, Fleekz, and Omgbarz round out a roster built on originality over algorithms.

Every track in the CWI catalog is co-produced by Black Lansky alongside the primary producer — a standing creative standard that gives the catalog its signature sonic identity.

Radio 365 — Streaming 24/7

Radio 365 is CWI's around-the-clock streaming station. The full catalog, roster artists, and curated alternative rap play continuously — no subscriptions, no signups, just press play. The station also powers the live experience and scheduled programming blocks.

Music, Film & Sync Licensing

CWI's music division handles releases, distribution, and artist development. The film division produces visual content including the upcoming Cyberpunk 2027 project. For sync licensing inquiries — film, TV, games, advertising — visit our contact page or email hp@cumulativeweb.com.

For Artists & Developers

CWI builds software for independents, including tools for distribution, analytics, and AI-assisted production. Artists interested in working with the label can explore services, upcoming events, and the model casting program. Machine-readable catalog data is available through our catalog API.
