Cumulative Web Inc — Independent Record Label

Cumulative Web Inc (CWI) is an independent record label based in the Washington DC metropolitan area, specializing in alternative rap and post-trap futurism. Founded by producer Black Lansky, the label develops artists, produces records, and licenses music for film, television, and games.

The Artist Roster

The CWI roster brings together some of the most distinctive voices in independent music. That Boy Hi Hat leads the flagship catalog with a genre-defying sound that has earned millions of streams. 183 Wilboi fuses rock energy with rap cadence. Jamaican artist Dre50 brings dancehall and afrobeats heat with records like "Pix" and "Vai Vem Vem." Greg Porn, King Ahkeem, Fleekz, and Omgbarz round out a roster built on originality over algorithms.

Every track in the CWI catalog is co-produced by Black Lansky alongside the primary producer — a standing creative standard that gives the catalog its signature sonic identity.

Radio 365 — Streaming 24/7

Radio 365 is CWI's around-the-clock streaming station. The full catalog, roster artists, and curated alternative rap play continuously — no subscriptions, no signups, just press play. The station also powers the live experience and scheduled programming blocks.

Music, Film & Sync Licensing

CWI's music division handles releases, distribution, and artist development. The film division produces visual content including the upcoming Cyberpunk 2027 project. For sync licensing inquiries — film, TV, games, advertising — visit our contact page or email hp@cumulativeweb.com.

For Artists & Developers

CWI builds software for independents, including tools for distribution, analytics, and AI-assisted production. Artists interested in working with the label can explore services, upcoming events, and the model casting program. Machine-readable catalog data is available through our catalog API.
