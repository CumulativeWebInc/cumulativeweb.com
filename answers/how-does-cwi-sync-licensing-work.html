How does CWI sync licensing work?

CWI sync licensing runs on direct clearance: the label manages the artists, so one signature clears both master and publishing — no publisher chase, no split-owner hunt. Licenses issue on the SAIL short form and clear in under five minutes.

The mechanics

CWI's 50/50 direct-clearance posture is owner-confirmed. Because the label manages the artists, every rights holder needed to close is at the table — one signature clears the whole song for qualifying placements.

The SAIL form

The license itself is the SAIL — Simple Agreement for Instant License v1.0 (MusicAtlas, CC BY-ND 4.0). Five fields, e-signatures accepted. The terms are also published machine-readable at the /license endpoint, for agents that read before the humans sign.

The flow

Find a track with the scored catalog search or the sync page. Email hp@cumulativeweb.com with the project, the track and the use. Sign the five-field form. Clearance in under five minutes.
