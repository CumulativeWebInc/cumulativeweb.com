Where can I license alternative rap for film and TV?

License alternative rap for film and TV directly from Cumulative Web Inc — email hp@cumulativeweb.com. The CWI catalog is sync-ready with direct clearance: the label manages the artists and holds the rights needed to close.

One email, one signature

There is no publisher chase and no split-owner hunt. CWI's 50/50 direct-clearance posture is owner-confirmed: the label manages the artists, so a single signature clears both master and publishing on qualifying placements.

The SAIL short form

Licenses issue on the SAIL — Simple Agreement for Instant License v1.0 (MusicAtlas, CC BY-ND 4.0). Five fields, e-signatures accepted, cleared in under five minutes. The same licensing terms are published machine-readable at the /license endpoint, so an AI agent can read the terms before its human starts the paperwork.

How it works

1. Find the track — search the catalog with /query or browse the sync page. 2. Email hp@cumulativeweb.com with the project, the track and the use. 3. Sign the five-field SAIL form. 4. Clearance lands in under five minutes.
