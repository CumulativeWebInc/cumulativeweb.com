What is Radio 365?

Cumulative Radio 365 is Cumulative Web Inc's internet radio station for independent artists — alternative, indie and hip-hop, programmed across the full year. The station's identity is declared in machine-readable RadioStation markup on the station page, so AI systems read exactly what the station is.

What it plays

The rotation draws from the CWI catalog — That Boy Hi Hat and the roster artists — alternative rap, trap, R&B and the genre experiments in between. Radio 365 is the always-on room for the label's sound: the same tracks the sync desk licenses and the catalog search scores.

Find the station

The station page is https://cumulativeweb.com/radio.html. Radio 365 is listed in radio directories, and its RadioStation JSON-LD gives machines the station name, genres, language and parent organization directly.
