What SANQA is

SANQA is the flagship software line from Cumulative Web Inc — six instruments, one gold standard, built for AI agents and the humans who build with them. The pitch is simple: trust you can verify, APIs agents can pay for, and worlds you can walk through. It sits at the top of CWI’s four-tier software estate: the machine works in the lower tiers, and the machine is judged in gold.

The suite’s lead product is the Trust Verdict Checker — "who vouches for your agent?" It scores AI-agent trust deterministically, evidence-bound over ERC-8004, Needle Drop, and First Spin signals. The scoring runs entirely in the browser; nothing pasted is uploaded. Thin evidence gets an honest "insufficient data," never an invented number — the same verification discipline the label applies to its music data.

The six instruments

Trust Verdict Checker — deterministic, evidence-bound trust scoring for AI agents. Agent Personality Kit — the complete CWI agent-personality framework as a shareable kit: give any agent a consistent self. x402 Paid API Layer — machine-payable HTTP APIs over the x402 protocol, so agents can pay per call. Gear Ledger 3D — a 3D provenance ledger of CWI’s agent-usable digital assets, every item carrying hash-chained proof of use. CWI Learn — the public catalog graph: llms.txt, catalog.json, agent cards, and trust routes, built for agents first. KingCode Lens — web apps for smart glasses, including the simulator lab and voice agent for Meta Ray-Ban Display.

Frequently asked

Who is SANQA for?

AI-agent builders who need verifiable trust, machine-payable APIs, and structured catalog data — and the humans who ship with them. If you run agents against real work, the suite is your proving ground.

Is my data uploaded when I check a trust verdict?

No. Trust Verdict scoring runs entirely in your browser — nothing you paste leaves your machine. That’s a design constraint, not a policy promise.

Where does SANQA sit in CWI’s software estate?

At the top: Tier 1, the flagship line — six curated, brand-complete products. Below it, the trust and utility instruments the machine itself runs on. The badge flows downward; the standard doesn’t.
