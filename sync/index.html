The SAIL one-sheet

We license on the SAIL — Simple Agreement for Instant License v1.0 (MusicAtlas, CC BY-ND 4.0) — a short-form sync license built for speed. Five fields, e-signatures accepted, clears in under 5 minutes.

1. Project
Your film, video, pitch, or campaign — what the music is syncing to.
2. Music / recording
The track (and version) you're licensing, picked below.
3. Rights covered
We check Both Master & Publishing — our 50/50 direct-clearance posture means one signature clears the whole song.
4. Fee
The license fee you propose. No hidden terms.
5. Paid-promo cap
The ceiling for paid social promotion of the project.

Full agreement text: SAIL v1.0 (PDF) · user guide (PDF). SAIL is MusicAtlas's open framework, not an industry standard — it's the fast path, and some counterparties' lawyers will want their own paper. That's fine too.
