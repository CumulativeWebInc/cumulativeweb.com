About this track

Warped & Wicked is That Boy Hi Hat plumbing the underground, an alternative rap track whose title Black Lansky corrected canonically on 2026-09-17, the same recording that appears on both albums. The production is dark and menacing, distorted 808s and stuttering hi-hats throughout with synth leads that bend and warp around the vocal. That Boy Hi Hat raps in a defiant, hypnotic cadence, his flow locking into the double-time shuffle of the drum machine while vocal chops flicker in the chorus. The mix is raw and brooding, reverb-heavy on the hook and deliberately heavy in the low end, a nocturnal rap record built for late-night drives. It sounds like the haunted twin of his melodic trap singles, recalling the danger and grit of early dark trap in the vein of the underground scene he came up through. For fans of cinematic rap, Warped & Wicked evokes a dystopian atmosphere without losing the aggressive forward motion of trap drums. It sits alongside the moodier entries in the CWI catalog, in conversation with the haunted records on the same two albums. Filtered synths, claps layered underneath the hook, and 808 bass carrying the whole frame make this one of the catalog's most hypnotic listens.
