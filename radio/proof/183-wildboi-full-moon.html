About this track

Full Moon is 183 Wildboi rising through the CWI roster, a rap and trap record co-produced with Black Lansky that premiered through the Rock-Rap Night session. The production is 808-driven and anthemic, booming 808s and trap drums throughout while synth pads glow layered underneath the verses. 183 Wildboi delivers with a confident, energetic flow, his vocals cutting over crisp hi-hats and a bassline that stays urgent from verse to chorus. The hook turns euphoric, claps and ad-libs stacking over the drums until the track sounds like a victory march for the CWI catalog. For fans of melodic rap, Full Moon recalls the high-ceiling energy of the label's flagship singles and sits alongside That Boy Hi Hat's trap records in conversation with the post-trap futurism wave. In the vein of stadium-sized hip hop, it evokes a sold-out night, triumphant, hype, and built to scale. Black Lansky's co-production fingerprints are all over the layering: rolling hi-hats in the chorus, atmospheric pads in the intro, sub bass carrying the bridge. As the track that pilots the weekly session-podcast engine, Full Moon is 183 Wildboi's confident arrival under his confirmed public name. Co-produced by Black Lansky.
