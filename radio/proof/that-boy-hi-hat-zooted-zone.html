About this track

The breakout record of That Boy Hi Hat's catalog, "Zooted Zone" is where his post trap sound went fully feral. Produced by Kokurcho and Black Lansky — written by That Boy Hi Hat and Black Lansky — the track moves at a blistering 163 BPM in C-sharp minor, and the tempo is the whole point: this is adrenaline rendered as audio. The production is synth-driven and bass-heavy, with crisp hi-hats skittering at double-time throughout, hard-hitting drums that hit like a kicked door, and an 808-driven low end rolling in massive, hypnotic waves. A synth lead cuts through the mix like a siren, and ad-libs stack thick in the chorus to keep the energy pinned. That Boy Hi Hat rides the beat with a locked-in, chant-like flow — bars about hedonism and street narrative, living large while angels and demons negotiate over the same bottle. His delivery is aggressive and anthemic, underground to the bone, with a late-night melodic drift that keeps it hypnotic rather than merely loud. For fans of high-velocity trap, it sounds like 2 AM with the exits sealed; in the vein of the great hype records, it recalls a night that refuses to end. Verified by the catalog at over 313,000 streams and held across curator playlists including Flow's "No Label Needed," "Zooted Zone" sits at the center of That Boy Hi Hat's catalog — the hinge everything after it extends.
