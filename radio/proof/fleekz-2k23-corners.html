About this track

Released in February 2023 as a single, "2K23 Corner$" is Fleekz's street-certified underground hip hop statement — with That Boy Hi Hat stepping in for a feature that turns the record into a crossroads moment. Fleekz sets the tone: a hungry, gritty delivery over trap drums and an 808-driven low end that keeps the whole mix menacing and late-night. That Boy Hi Hat's verse arrives like a reminder of who's in the room, treating the corner as both setting and state of mind. The production is lean by design: crisp hi-hats and hard-hitting drums throughout, a bassline that rolls underneath rather than overpowers, and synth stabs layered into the chorus for extra tension. The mix is polished but keeps the raw edge intact — a record built for rooms where the bass does the talking. It sounds like 2023 underground hip hop at its most direct: in the vein of the street-narrative records that built the lane, and for fans of aggressive, confident rap with no filler. "2K23 Corner$" recalls the era when a feature verse could redraw an artist's map — and for Fleekz, this collaboration with That Boy Hi Hat did exactly that. As one of the earliest links between That Boy Hi Hat and the wider CWI orbit, this February 2023 single still hits like a time capsule of the underground.
