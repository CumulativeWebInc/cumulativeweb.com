About this track

Phantasm is a That Boy Hi Hat deep cut from Idol or Icon, the 2021 project that anchors the early catalog, with its vault alt master repaired in 2026 through a limiter pass that tamed true-peak clipping down to -0.5 dBTP. The record is dark and dystopian, synth-driven production built on arps and pads that drift throughout like a signal from a dead broadcast. That Boy Hi Hat raps in an introspective, brooding cadence, his vocal low in the mix against sub bass and kick drum patterns that hit with halftime menace. The hook turns ethereal, pitched vocals and atmospheric pads floating over drums that pull back to let the melody breathe, mysterious, nocturnal, and unmistakably cinematic. It sounds like the ancestor of his later futuristic records, recalling the melancholic synthwave of underground electronic rap in the vein of the post-trap futurism wave he would come to define. For fans of dark trap, Phantasm evokes a haunted future, lonely, cold, and hypnotic. It sits alongside the catalog's moodiest material, in conversation with the dystopian records that followed it on Idol or Icon. Synthesizer, 808 bass, hi-hats, and percussion all serve the atmosphere, and the repaired master lets the low end speak without the distortion that once clouded it. Produced by Jeck da General with Black Lansky co-producing.
