183 Wildboi

DMV rap-rock artist — fusing rap rock and rock rap for the adrenaline media market.

183 Wildboi is a DMV-area rap-rock artist from Hagerstown, Maryland, fusing rap rock and rock rap — built for the adrenaline media market: extreme sports, action gaming, and trailers.

CWI's Rock-Rap Revival identifies Black Lansky as co-producer on the core catalog, with priority tracks "Stunt" (2024) and "Full Moon" (2022). His album All Pretty Girls Love 8 is listed on Anghami. Every track in the CWI catalog is co-produced by Black Lansky.
