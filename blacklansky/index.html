Black Lansky

Artist, author, manager, producer, software and game builder.

Black Lansky is a multi-disciplinary creator: recording artist, author, artist manager, music producer, software builder, game developer, film editor, and creative designer. He runs Cumulative Web Inc.

He authored THE LOGO EFFECT: How AI and Structured Data Took an Indie Artist to Global Alt-Rap Dominance (published December 2025), the blueprint documenting the system he built at CWI.

As a manager and producer he guides alternative rap artist That Boy Hi Hat and carries production and co-production credits across the entire CWI catalog — every track in the catalog is co-produced by Black Lansky. Credits include That Boy Hi Hat's "Diabolique" and Idol or Icon (with Jeck Da General), King Akeem's singles, Greg Porn's "Pay Yourself" and "On Edge," Dre50's "Pix," and 183 Wildboi's "Stunt" and "Full Moon.
