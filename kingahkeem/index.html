King Akeem

Miami trap-rap artist — CWI's Global Alt-Rap Protocol.

King Akeem is a Miami-based trap-rap artist on the Cumulative Web Inc roster and a central voice in CWI's Global Alt-Rap Protocol — the company's framework for carrying alternative rap past its regional origins and into a worldwide circuit of radio, discovery engines, and AI-to-AI surfaces. Black Lansky named him on September 14, 2026 as one of the artists who "has music with" him, and King Akeem entered the CWI catalog through the Next Generation Protocol: seven singles, each a Black Lansky co-production, each carrying the Cumulative Web Inc label line.

His biggest record, "Star," has logged 112,510 plays — the largest single-track spike in the CWI catalog outside of That Boy Hi Hat's own numbers. Five of his masters were published to Radio 365 on October 4, 2026.

Biography

King Akeem records out of Miami, Florida, and his catalog sits at the intersection Black Lansky named in his own words on October 4, 2026: trap music, alternative music. The trap half is structural — rolling hi-hat programming, deep sub-bass pressure, minor-key atmosphere, the Miami trap-rap architecture built for car systems and club PAs. The alternative half is in the choices layered on top: cover art that treats each single as its own visual artifact, track concepts that reach outside street-rap convention, and a release cadence that behaves more like an art project than a street tape. That duality is the Global Alt-Rap Protocol in miniature — rap that reads as trap in the room and as alternative on the page.

A note on the name, because the record carries two spellings and both are honest. This page uses "King Akeem," following Black Lansky's correction of September 14, 2026, in which he called the "Ahkeem" spelling a distributor error. The official single artwork, however, spells the name "King Ahkeem" — the "EMBIID by KING AHKEEM" $100-bill art, the "KA / Element" periodic-table single art, and the covers across the catalog all carry the Ahkeem spelling. Black's own messages on October 4, 2026 write it lowercase — "king ahkeem" — so the variant lives in CWI's internal records as well as on the distributor's side. Both spellings name the same Miami artist. This page reflects "King Akeem" per the current site and records the "King Ahkeem" artwork variant for the archive.

Black's real photo of the artist went live on cumulativeweb.com on September 24, 2026 — the portrait shown at the top of this page. It anchors the catalog entry the way the masters anchor the music: a verified image for a verified artist, inside the same discovery engine that serves the tracks. Every fact on this page traces to CWI's verified record; nothing here is invented, and where a public credit is still pending verification, the page says so.

The Star Story

"Star" is King Akeem's biggest record by a distance — 112,510 plays, the largest single-track spike in the CWI catalog outside of That Boy Hi Hat's own numbers. On September 15, 2026, the figure was flagged inside CWI as a possible second story: a record from outside the flagship catalog pulling six-figure plays on its own gravity, with no campaign behind it. As of this writing, that story remains unexplored — no rollout has been built around it, no dissection published, no marketing machinery pointed at the number. The plays happened first; the narrative is still unwritten.

The master arrived at CWI on October 4, 2026 as part of the Radio 365 publication wave: 171.0 seconds, verified by ffprobe, paired with its artist-photo cover art pulled from the single release per Black's standing artwork order. At just under three minutes it is the longest of the opening four masters and the anchor of the batch — the record the rest of the catalog orbits, and the cover that puts the artist's own face on the flagship number.

Why the number matters is a question of context. Inside CWI, the Next Generation Protocol is the pipeline carrying artists beyond the flagship catalog — and "Star" is its first six-figure signal: a Miami trap-rap record at 112,510 plays with no campaign behind it. The plays are not a campaign result; they are a signal waiting for its campaign, and the flag raised on September 15 is still flying.

Radio 365

On October 4, 2026, five King Akeem masters were published to Radio 365. The first four — "Star," "Super Solid," "Embiid," and "Bluefaces 3.0" — arrived in the 5-track batch; "Element" followed in wave 2. The publication closed the loop between the catalog on paper and the catalog on air: every master that hit the stream is the same file CWI holds, verified and art-matched, playing around the clock.

Verification was mechanical and exact. Each master was run through ffprobe before publication — "Star" at 171.0 seconds, "Super Solid" at 125.9, "Embiid" at 124.1, "Bluefaces 3.0" at 144.5, "Element" at 193.5. No estimated durations, no distributor-supplied runtimes: the numbers on this page are the numbers the files themselves report, and they are the numbers the stream plays.

Cover art was matched per Black's standing order: radio artwork always comes from the single release, never the track or album URL. The five covers that went live — the artist photo for "Star," the lattice art for "Super Solid," the "EMBIID by KING AHKEEM" $100-bill art, the Medusa art for "Bluefaces 3.0," and the official "KA / Element" periodic-table single art — were each pulled from the corresponding single release. That rule is what keeps the artwork spelling "King Ahkeem" visible on the stream even as the page carries "King Akeem": the art is the distributor's record, the page is CWI's, and both are shown as they are.
