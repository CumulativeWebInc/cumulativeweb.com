# AI Agent Entry Point — Cumulative Web Inc / CWI Discovery Engine

Sync-licensing discovery for AI agents: natural-language brief in, licensable tracks with verified ISRCs and one-signature clearance out. This is NOT a general music metadata database — it is the AI surface for finding music you can license.
