That Boy Hi Hat

CWI flagship alternative rap artist — the originator of Post-Trap Futurism.

That Boy Hi Hat is the flagship alternative rap artist of Cumulative Web Inc, based in Frederick, MD. He is the originator of Post-Trap Futurism — the genre fusing alternative rap with futuristic production.

His verified catalog includes albums PTF: The Logo Effect (2025), The Alternative Theory (2025), and Idol or Icon (2021, co-produced with Jeck Da General). Breakout single "Zooted Zone" and "Doves & Diamonds" anchor the catalog, alongside "Flamerz" (produced by Jeck Da General), "Diabolique" (produced by Hybrid, 2026-07-03, recorded at Cue Recording Studio, Arlington VA), "Shaka Zulu," "Flex My Flame," "7th Angel," and "Golden Diamond."

Every track in the CWI catalog is co-produced by Black Lansky.
