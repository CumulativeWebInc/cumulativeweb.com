OMG Barz

CWI roster artist — warrior storyteller from the streets of New York.

OMG Barz is a CWI roster artist. Born into the aftermath of Black Power & Unity — a warrior, surviving a broken New York foster care system, a product of the American prison system. Surviving only by using a God-given ability to move men. Driven by his passion for music and a desire for greatness, his craft was perfected on the streets.

Every track in the CWI catalog is co-produced by Black Lansky.
