Services for artists

Two services we built for our own catalog — now open to independent artists. Proof, not promises: every claim in every report is verified or marked unverified.

  2. A CWI operator replies with your order ID and payment instructions. You pay only after you receive the invoice.
  3. We deliver — PDF + machine-readable JSON, human-reviewed, on the timeframe stated. Full refund if we're late.

The services

$49

CWI verifies your track's playlist placements and hands you the receipts — the same verification pipeline we run daily on our own catalog.

Sync Readiness Pack

$149

An audit of your catalog for sync readiness — the same checklist our own catalog passes before any pitch leaves the building.

  • Ownership & credits audit: master ownership, publishing administration, PRO affiliation — verified vs. flagged as needing your documents
  • Metadata & identifier gaps: ISRCs, credits, label copy — every gap disclosed, none hidden
  • Deliverables check: clean/FCC-safe edits, instrumentals, stems — what's ready, what's missing
  • Sample & clearance flags: anything that needs your paperwork before a license can close
  • Pitch-materials review: what a supervisor sees today, and what would make it land

Delivered in 5 business days — or a full refund, no questions. This pack assesses readiness; it is not a rights clearance and not legal advice.
